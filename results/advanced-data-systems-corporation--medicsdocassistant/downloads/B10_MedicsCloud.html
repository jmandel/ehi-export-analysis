§ 170.315 (b)(10) Electronic Health Information export

MedicsCloud authorized users can generate the Electronic Health Information Export (EHI) for a single patient and also the patient population in both HL7 CCDA xml format that comply with USCDI v1 requirements standards and HL7 FHIR v 4.0.1 US Core v 3.1.1.

  1. The Implementation guide for the HL7 CCDA xml standard can be accessed from below link.
    https://www.hl7.org/implement/standards/product_brief.cfm?product_id=492
  2. The implementation guide for HL7 FHIR® US Core Implementation Guide STU3 Release 3.1.1 can be accessed from below link.
    https://hl7.org/fhir/us/core/STU3.1.1/
