Costs and Disclosures

Costs are billed per account created for each end-user. End-users may include doctors, physicians, clinicians, and authorized technicians who use Moyae in a production setting. Testing accounts are not billed.

Additional costs per prescriber per month is necessary to enable e-prescribing and direct-messaging capabilities.

Initiation costs may be incurred for significant data migrations and onboarding and will be disclosed in final contracts.
