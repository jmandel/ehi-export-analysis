# Qualifacts Systems, LLC — Product Research

Researched: 2026-02-14
Developer website: https://www.qualifacts.com

## Overview

Qualifacts Systems, LLC is one of the largest providers of SaaS-based Electronic Health Records for the behavioral health and human services market. Based in Nashville, Tennessee, the company was formed through the 2020 merger of Qualifacts (which offered the CareLogic platform) and Credible Behavioral Health (founded in 2000), orchestrated by private equity firms Warburg Pincus and Martis Capital. Qualifacts subsequently acquired InSync (December 2021) and OnCall Health (November 2022, a Canadian telehealth/patient portal company), assembling a portfolio of four distinct EHR platforms under one umbrella.

The combined company claims over 2,500 customers representing 75,000 providers serving more than 6 million patients across all 50 states. Qualifacts says it serves one-third of participating Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic (CCBHC) agencies nationally, with over 170 CCBHC customers as of late 2023. Qualifacts was acquired by Warburg Pincus in 2019 for $300M, and later received majority investment from Great Hill Partners. The company holds the #1 ranking in the KLAS Best in Software and Professional Services report for Behavioral Health.

Qualifacts has four ONC-certified EHR platforms: **CareLogic** (enterprise-focused), **Credible** (large agency/enterprise), **InSync** (small-to-mid practice), and **OnCall** (virtual care). Each serves a distinct segment of the behavioral health market.

### What It Is

Credible Behavioral Health is a comprehensive, web-based (cloud-hosted, SaaS) EHR platform designed specifically for behavioral health and human services organizations. It is a full-featured EHR — not a component of a larger product — encompassing clinical documentation, billing, scheduling, treatment planning, ePrescribing, client engagement, analytics, and administrative functions in an integrated platform. The platform has been in use for over 20 years (originally from Credible Behavioral Health Inc., founded 2000, which merged with Qualifacts in 2020).

The certified module covers a broad range of ONC criteria (40+ certified criteria spanning clinical data [(a)(1)–(a)(5), (a)(12), (a)(14)–(a)(15)], care coordination [(b)(1)–(b)(3), (b)(8), (b)(10)–(b)(11)], patient portal [(e)(1), (e)(3)], clinical quality measures [(c)(1)–(c)(4)], public health reporting [(f)(1)–(f)(2), (f)(5)], and FHIR APIs [(g)(7), (g)(9)–(g)(10)]). The SED intended user description is "Outpatient Clinic."

### Users & Market

Credible targets large enterprise agencies, multi-site behavioral health organizations, and Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinics (CCBHCs). The platform supports community-based outpatient, residential, and inpatient care settings.

**Typical users include:**
- Therapists, psychiatrists, and behavioral health clinicians
- Clinical supervisors and program directors
- Billing staff and revenue cycle managers
- System administrators
- Intake coordinators and case managers
- Clients/patients (via the patient portal)

**Settings served:**
- Community mental health centers
- Addiction/substance use treatment programs
- Intellectual/Developmental Disability (I/DD) services
- Health and human services agencies
- Residential treatment facilities
- County behavioral health departments (notably multiple California counties chose Qualifacts)
- CCBHCs

Qualifacts claims the Credible platform is ranked #1 in KLAS for Behavioral Health Software and Professional Services. An 80% user satisfaction rating was reported based on 96 reviews across major review sites.

### Modules & Functionality

Based on vendor materials and review sites, Credible offers the following major modules and capabilities:

**Clinical Documentation & Assessment**
- Configurable clinical forms and documentation workflows
- Integrated assessment tools: Wiley Treatment Planners, CANS (Child and Adolescent Needs and Strengths), ASAM (American Society of Addiction Medicine), PHQ-9, DLA-20, and more
- Treatment planning with outcome alignment
- Progress notes / therapy session documentation
- Clinical decision support with evidence-based treatment suggestions, preventive care reminders, drug interaction alerts
- 60+ configurable alert types for compliance and quality tracking

**ePrescribing & Medication Management**
- Electronic prescribing including EPCS (electronic prescribing of controlled substances)
- PDMP (Prescription Drug Monitoring Program) integration
- Medication management and adherence tracking
- Drug interaction checking

**Scheduling & Intake**
- Appointment scheduling with provider availability/specialty matching
- Intake workflows
- Appointment reminders to reduce no-show rates
- In-person and virtual appointment scheduling

**Billing & Revenue Cycle Management**
- Integrated HIPAA-compliant billing
- Claim scrubbing and denial management queues
- Batch claim error reporting
- Configurable billing rules for various payment models and value-based care
- State-specific billing requirements support
- Revenue cycle management
- Credentialing services
- Claims submission to payers

**Residential & Inpatient Management**
- Facility whiteboard for shift-to-shift consistency
- Duty log tracking
- Room assignment and transfer management
- Census reporting dashboards
- Virtual representation of inpatient/residential clients

**Client Engagement & Telehealth**
- HIPAA-compliant, white-labeled patient/client portal
- Telehealth / video visits supporting groups up to 50 participants (via OnCall integration)
- Secure messaging
- Content delivery to clients through the portal

**Analytics & Reporting**
- Customizable reporting dashboards with graphical capabilities
- BI Dashboard Library for organizational metrics
- Advanced search across multiple data fields
- Reports covering financial performance, staffing, program performance, client outcomes
- BI Connector for data integration with external systems

**Interoperability & Data Exchange**
- Connection modules for sending demographic and clinical data to HIEs
- FHIR R4 API support for 21st Century Cures Act compliance
- Integration with state HIEs, labs, hospitals, and primary care providers
- OneQ integration layer for connecting credentialing, MAT, primary care, RCM, BI solutions
- Over 80 integrations available through the Qualifacts integration marketplace
- Public health reporting capabilities (immunization registries, syndromic surveillance, electronic case reporting per certified criteria)

**Administration & Security**
- Self-service system configuration (admin can modify workflows, forms, programs without vendor)
- SSL encryption, two-factor/multi-factor authentication, IP whitelisting
- Single Sign-On (SSO) integration
- Role-based access controls
- Client data restriction for sensitive accounts
- SOC2-compliant password policies
- Auto-save and draft recovery features

**AI-Powered Features**
- Qualifacts iQ: AI-powered clinical documentation that generates notes from session transcripts
- Measurement-based care tools

**Mobile Access**
- HIPAA-compliant mobile application for community-based and field-based care

### Data & Content

Based on the features and modules described, Credible stores and manages the following data categories:

**Clinical Data** (strongly evidenced by certified criteria and vendor materials):
- Patient/client demographics and contact information
- Clinical assessments and screening results (PHQ-9, CANS, ASAM, DLA-20, etc.)
- Treatment plans and goals with outcome tracking
- Progress notes and session documentation
- Problem lists, medication lists, medication allergy lists (certified criteria a)(1)–(a)(3))
- CPOE / clinical orders (certified criterion (a)(4))
- Clinical decision support data (certified criterion (a)(5))
- Medication prescriptions (ePrescribing, EPCS)
- Drug interaction alerts
- Vitals/vital signs (implied by clinical criteria)
- Immunization records (certified for immunization registry reporting (f)(1))
- Lab orders and results (implied by interoperability with labs)

**Administrative & Operational Data**:
- Scheduling / appointment data
- Intake records
- Facility/residential census and room assignments
- Duty logs and shift records
- Provider/staff information (specialties, availability, credentials)
- Program and location configurations

**Billing & Financial Data**:
- Insurance/payer information
- Claims data (submissions, denials, scrubbing results)
- Payment and receivables records
- Authorization tracking
- Credentialing records

**Communication & Engagement Data**:
- Client portal messages and communications
- Telehealth session records
- Appointment reminders and notifications

**Reporting & Analytics Data**:
- Aggregated outcome measures
- Quality metrics (CQMs per certified criteria)
- Financial/operational dashboards
- Syndromic surveillance and public health reporting data

**Interoperability/Exchange Data**:
- CCD/CCDA documents (transitions of care, per (b)(1)–(b)(3))
- HIE data exchange records
- FHIR API access logs

**Notable**: The vendor's website and materials clearly describe integrated billing with claim scrubbing (indicating claims/financial data is stored within the system, not outsourced). The ePrescribing/EPCS capability confirms medication and prescription data. The residential/inpatient management features confirm census, room assignment, and facility operations data. The 60+ alert types and clinical decision support confirm that alert configurations and clinical rules data are stored. The AI documentation feature (Qualifacts iQ) implies session transcript/audio data may also be stored.
