Documentation for HC_PtBal

Health Care Data for Patient Balance

Original Column Name Expanded Column Name Description
PtBalID Patient Balance Details Unique identifier for each patient balance record, detailing financial transactions and balances for patient accounts.
PtID Patient Details Unique identifier for the patient, linking to their personal and medical information within the EHR system.
BalIns Balance Insurance Total outstanding balance owed by insurance companies for the patient's medical care.
BalPt Balance Patient Total outstanding balance owed by the patient for their medical care.
BalIns0_30 Balance Insurance 0-30 Days Insurance balance amount that is 0 to 30 days old, indicating recent insurance claims or payments.
BalIns31_60 Balance Insurance 31-60 Days Insurance balance amount that is 31 to 60 days old, showing pending or overdue payments from insurance providers.
BalIns61_90 Balance Insurance 61-90 Days Insurance balance amount that is 61 to 90 days old, highlighting potentially delinquent insurance payments.
BalIns91_ Balance Insurance Over 91 Days Insurance balance amount that is over 91 days old, indicating significantly overdue payments from insurance.
BalPt0_30 Balance Patient 0-30 Days Patient balance amount that is 0 to 30 days old, representing recent charges or payments by the patient.
BalPt31_60 Balance Patient 31-60 Days Patient balance amount that is 31 to 60 days old, indicating pending or overdue payments by the patient.
BalPt61_90 Balance Patient 61-90 Days Patient balance amount that is 61 to 90 days old, suggesting potential delinquencies in patient payments.
BalPt91_ Balance Patient Over 91 Days Patient balance amount that is over 91 days old, showing significantly overdue payments by the patient.
DateLastStmt Date Last Statement The date of the last financial statement issued, either to the patient or insurance, regarding their balance.
IsUndistPayIns Is Undistributed Pay Insurance Indicates whether there are undistributed or unallocated insurance payments in the patient's account.
IsUndistPayPt Is Undistributed Pay Patient Indicates whether there are undistributed or unallocated patient payments in the patient's account.
LastInsPaymentID Last Insurance Payment Details Identifier for the most recent insurance payment made for the patient's account, useful for tracking and reconciliation.
LastPtPaymentID Last Patient Payment Details Identifier for the most recent payment made by the patient, important for financial tracking and account management.
SiteID Site Details Identifier for the healthcare facility or site where the financial transactions or patient care occurred.
ZUChkSum ZU Checksum Checksum value used for audit logs, ensuring data integrity and tracking changes in the patient balance records.
ZUDate ZU Date Date stamp used in audit logs, indicating when entries related to patient balances were made or modified.
ZULogonHisID ZU Logon History Details Identifier for logon history in the audit logs, potentially linking user access and actions to specific patient balance records.
