Documentation for HC_ContOfCare.csv

Health Care Data for Continuity of Care

Original Column Name Expanded Column Name Description
ContOfCareID Continuity of Care Details Unique identifier for each continuity of care record, critical for tracking patient care transitions and ongoing treatment plans.
DateCreated Date Created The date on which the continuity of care document or record was created.
Document Document The actual content of the continuity of care document, which may include treatment plans, care transitions, and other relevant information.
DocID Document Details Unique identifier for the specific document related to continuity of care.
EntityFrom Entity From The originating entity (e.g., hospital, clinic) from which the continuity of care record is transferred.
EntityTo Entity To The receiving entity (e.g., another hospital, a rehabilitation center) to which the continuity of care record is directed.
Html HTML Indicates if the document is available in HTML format, useful for electronic sharing and viewing.
IsCCR Is Continuity of Care Record Specifies whether the document is a Continuity of Care Record, a standardized form for sharing patient information.
IsExport Is Export Indicates whether the continuity of care document is flagged for export to another system or entity.
PrivacyCode Privacy Code A code indicating the level of privacy or confidentiality assigned to the continuity of care record.
PtID Patient Details Unique identifier for the patient whose continuity of care is being documented and tracked.
Purpose Purpose The intended purpose or reason for creating and sharing the continuity of care document.
RefID Reference Details Identifier for additional references or related documents linked to the continuity of care record.
Version Version The version of the continuity of care document, important for ensuring the most current information is used.
ZUChkSum ZU Checksum Audit log tracking checksum value, ensuring the integrity and accuracy of the continuity of care data.
ZUDate ZU Date Date indicating when the continuity of care record was last accessed or modified, used for audit log tracking.
ZULogonHisID ZU Logon History Details Identifier for the history of user logon activities related to the continuity of care document, for security and audit purposes.
PrvName Provider Name Name of the healthcare provider or entity responsible for the patient's continuity of care.
