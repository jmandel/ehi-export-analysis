Data

Last updated: 17 Jan 2025

The data module provides you with data to compute on. It provides curated, secure access to both PHI (e.g. patient data) and non-PHI (e.g. staff and practice-level data), representing the current state of your target Canvas instance. The module’s classes offer convenience methods and operators that make business logic and clinical logic easy to express with standard terminologies like ICD-10, SNOMED-CT, CPT, and the like.

Data module classes are Django ORM models, which allow easy retrieval of data at runtime through Django’s expressive QuerySet API.

The pages below provide listings of the models, their attributes, and examples of usage.

AllergyIntolerance

Harmful or undesired physiological responses associated with exposure to a substance.

Application

A plugin application.

Appointment

A scheduled meeting between a patient and a provider.

Assessment

Clinical assessment of a patient's condition.

BannerAlert

An alert notification linked to a patient.

BillingLineItem

A billable code linked to a patient note.

BusinessLine

A group of patients that share a common brand under an organization.

Calendar

Calendars associated with providers.

CanvasUser

User accounts associated with other records.

CareTeam

Teams assigned for patient care.

ChargeDescriptionMaster

Billing charges in Canvas that can be added to the note footer.

Claim

A healthcare claim.

Command

Structured units of documentation in a patient's chart.

CommonEnumerationTypes

Common choice classes used in multiple models.

CompoundMedication

Compound medications, which are custom-made medications tailored to a patient's specific needs.

Condition

Condition, diagnosis, or reason for seeking medical attention.

Coverage

Patient insurance coverage.

DetectedIssue

Actual or potential clinical issue with or between one or more active or proposed clinical actions for a patient.

Device

Type of a manufactured item that is used in the provision of healthcare.

EligibilitySummary

Summary of copay and coinsurance for a Coverage.

Encounter

A patient Encounter connected to a Note in Canvas.

ExternalEvent

External clinical events from ADT feeds such as admissions, discharges, and transfers.

Facility

A location where healthcare services are provided.

Imaging

Analysis of imaging tests to obtain information about the health of a patient.

Immunization

A record of a vaccination that is being administered to a patient, either now, in the past, or in the future.

Labs

Analysis of clinical specimens to obtain information about the health of a patient.

LabPartner

Lab partners and the tests they offer within Canvas.

Letter

Patient correspondence letters created within Canvas.

Medication

A record of a medication that is being consumed by a patient, either now, in the past, or in the future.

Medication History

A record of a patient's medication history, including medications that were taken in the past but are no longer active.

Medication Statement

A record of a medication statement by a patient from the past.

Message

Messages sent to and from Canvas.

Note

Clinical notes on patient charts.

Observation

Measurements and simple assertions made about a patient.

Organization

The clinical organization present in the Canvas EMR.

Patient

Data used to categorize individuals for identification, records matching, and other purposes.

PatientConsent

Documented patient consents that ensure legal compliance and protect patient rights.

PayorSpecificCharge

A billing charge specific to a certain transactor in Canvas.

Posting

Payments and postings associated with healthcare claims.

PracticeLocation

The practice locations present in the Canvas EMR.

ProtocolCurrent

The current state of clinical protocols applied to patients within Canvas.

ProtocolOverride

A record of a protocol being snoozed for a patient.

Stop Medication Event

A record of a Stop Medication Event, when a medication is removed from a patient's medication list.

Questionnaire

Groups of coded questions and the coded patient responses.

ReasonForVisit

The reason for a patient's visit.

Referral

A referral directing a specific patient to another provider or specialist.

ServiceProvider

Data associated with Service Providers.

Staff

Data associated with Staff members.

Task

Data associated with Tasks.

Team

Data associated with Teams.

Uncategorized Clinical Documents

Data associated with uncategorized clinical documents and their reviews.

ValueSets

Lists of codes and terms from various clinical coding systems grouped by a defining concept.
