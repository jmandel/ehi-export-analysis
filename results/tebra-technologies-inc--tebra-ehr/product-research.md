# Tebra Technologies, Inc. — Product Research

Researched: 2026-02-15
Developer website: https://www.tebra.com/

## Overview

Tebra Technologies, Inc. is a privately held healthcare technology company headquartered in Southern California, formed in November 2021 through the merger of **Kareo** (cloud-based clinical and financial software for independent practices, founded by Dan Rodrigues) and **PatientPop** (practice growth and patient experience technology, co-founded by Luke Kervin and Travis Schneider). The combined company rebranded as "Tebra" and completed its brand unification in April 2024 when the PatientPop website was fully integrated into tebra.com.

Tebra positions itself as the "only all-in-one EHR+ platform built exclusively for independent healthcare practices." The company serves over **140,000 private healthcare providers** and manages over **125 million patient records**. At the time of the merger, the combined company had approximately 1,000 employees. In December 2025, Tebra secured **$250 million** in new equity and debt financing (led by Hildred, with J.P. Morgan providing the debt facility), over-subscribed with participation from existing investors Toba Capital, Transformation Capital, and HLM Venture Partners. A prior $72 million round had reportedly pushed the company's valuation above $1 billion. The company's target market is squarely **ambulatory independent/private practices** across a range of specialties including primary care, mental health, pediatrics, dermatology, chiropractic, podiatry, and others. It also serves billing companies that manage multiple practices.

## Product: Tebra EHR (EHR+ Platform)

CHPL ID: 11557

### What It Is

Tebra EHR is a cloud-based, all-in-one practice management and electronic health records platform designed for independent and small-to-mid-size ambulatory practices. The certified module is "Tebra EHR" (v5.1, certified December 19, 2024), but it is part of a broader integrated platform the company brands as "EHR+" — which encompasses clinical documentation (EHR), billing and revenue cycle management, patient experience/engagement, practice marketing, and AI-powered automation tools, all accessible through "one login, one platform."

The product's lineage traces to **Kareo Clinical** (the EHR component) and **Kareo Billing** (the practice management/billing component), which were already integrated products before the Tebra rebrand. The PatientPop merger added the practice marketing and patient experience growth tools. Today, Tebra markets the combined platform as a single product suite.

The CHPL certification covers a broad set of criteria: clinical data (a)(1)–(a)(5), (a)(12), (a)(14); transitions of care (b)(1)–(b)(3); EHI export (b)(10)–(b)(11); patient portal/VDT (e)(1), (e)(3); public health — cancer case reporting (f)(5); and FHIR APIs (g)(2)–(g)(7), (g)(9)–(g)(10). Additional software dependencies listed in the certification include **LabSoft** (lab integration), **Updox** (health information exchange/faxing), **DrFirst Rcopia** (e-prescribing), and **SmileCDR** (FHIR server).

### Users & Market

- **Target users**: Ambulatory clinicians (physicians, NPs, PAs), practice managers, billing staff, front-desk staff, and patients (via portal). The CHPL metadata describes intended users as "Ambulatory clinicians and staff."
- **Settings**: Independent/private practices, solo practitioners, small-to-mid-size group practices, and billing companies. Not targeted at hospitals, health systems, or enterprise organizations.
- **Specialties**: Primary care, family medicine, internal medicine, mental/behavioral health, pediatrics, dermatology, chiropractic, podiatry, OB/GYN, and others. The product is generalist-ambulatory, not specialty-locked.
- **Scale**: 140,000+ providers, 125M+ patient records. The company highlights ratings of 4.6/5 on Trustpilot and 4.1/5 on G2, and 3.9/5 on Capterra (1,311 reviews).
- **Deployment**: Cloud-hosted (SaaS). The billing module has historically required a desktop application download for some functions, which reviewers have noted as a pain point.

### Modules & Functionality

Based on vendor website, feature pages, mandatory disclosures, press releases, and third-party reviews, Tebra's platform includes the following major functional areas:

**Clinical Documentation / EHR**
- SOAP note charting with pre-built templates, macros, and text shortcuts
- **AI Note Assist**: ambient AI documentation that reduces note-taking time (vendor claims 60% reduction per note; over 500,000 notes generated by customers). HIPAA-compliant.
- **SALT** (pull-forward past notes): allows pulling forward prior visit content into new notes
- Problem lists, medication lists, allergy lists
- Patient Facesheet showing medications, allergies, complaints, vitals, medical history, and recent lab results
- Clinical decision support and drug interaction checking
- Visit summaries and care plans

**E-Prescribing**
- Powered by **DrFirst Rcopia** (third-party integration)
- Prescription ordering with drug interaction checks, refill tracking
- Integrates with **Surescripts** for prescription routing

**Electronic Labs**
- Lab ordering and results management
- Bidirectional interfaces with **LabCorp** and **Quest Diagnostics**
- Integration via **LabSoft**
- eLab results viewable by patients in the portal

**Telehealth**
- Built-in HIPAA/HITRUST-certified video visits
- Group appointments (up to 200 participants)
- Dedicated provider URLs, virtual waiting rooms
- No separate login required

**Scheduling & Appointments**
- Provider scheduling with calendar management
- Online patient self-scheduling (book or request)
- Automated appointment reminders via text, email, and phone
- Two-way text communication with patients

**Patient Intake**
- Digital intake forms completed online before visits
- Data flows directly into the EHR to reduce manual entry

**Patient Portal**
- 24/7 patient access to visit summaries, test results, lab results, medications, medical history
- Secure two-way messaging between patients and providers
- Treatment plan viewing and e-signing
- Patient education materials and assessments
- Bill viewing, online payments, and transaction history
- Guest/caregiver access authorization
- Document sharing (send and receive health records)

**Billing & Revenue Cycle Management**
- Insurance eligibility verification in real-time via ANSI X12 270/271 queries to 2,700+ payers
- Electronic claim submission with automatic error detection, payer-specific rules, and batch processing
- ERA/EOB auto-posting via RPA (robotic process automation)
- Denial tracking and management
- Automated patient statements (paper and electronic) with QR codes
- Patient payment collection: card-on-file, online, in-person, email/text payment links
- Accounts receivable tracking and reporting
- Customizable dashboards for denials, reimbursements, and collections
- Charge capture and coding support
- The billing module originated as **Kareo Billing** and some reviewers note it requires a desktop application for certain billing functions

**Practice Marketing** (from PatientPop lineage)
- Custom practice websites with SEO optimization
- Paid search marketing management
- Online reputation management: review monitoring across platforms, AI Review Replies, AI Review Insights
- Social media marketing
- Directory profile management across 80+ platforms

**Reporting & Analytics**
- Quality dashboard for MIPS reporting and tracking
- Medicare Promoting Interoperability monitoring
- Customizable billing/revenue dashboards
- Automated MIPS reporting (no additional documentation entry required per vendor)

**Interoperability & Data Exchange**
- C-CDA document generation and exchange (transitions of care)
- FHIR R4 APIs (certified under (g)(10))
- Health information exchange via **Updox**
- Cancer case reporting (f)(5)
- EHI export capability: C-CDA, PDF, and native document formats (per mandatory disclosures)

**Notable observations**:
- The mandatory disclosures page states Tebra Clinical enables providers to "export out EHI for individual patients and their patient population at any time without developer assistance" in C-CDA, PDF, and native document formats.
- Third-party reviews on Capterra note that data export/migration away from Tebra can be costly ("potentially costing thousands just to obtain your own data"), suggesting the EHI export process may have practical friction despite regulatory requirements.
- The reliance on third-party components (DrFirst for e-prescribing, LabSoft for labs, Updox for HIE, SmileCDR for FHIR) means some data may flow through or be stored in these partner systems. Whether EHI export covers data held by these integrated systems is an important question for Phase 2 analysis.
- The billing module's desktop application requirement (noted by reviewers) suggests a potentially different data architecture for billing vs. clinical data.
- The website does not mention imaging/radiology ordering or results as a distinct feature, though it is referenced in passing on one page ("Order prescriptions, lab tests, and imaging from one platform"). The depth of imaging data stored is unclear.
