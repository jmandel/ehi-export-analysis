# Nextech — Product Research

Researched: 2026-02-16
Developer website: https://www.nextech.com

## Overview

Nextech is a specialty-focused EHR and practice management vendor founded in 1997 and headquartered in Tampa, Florida. The company serves dermatology, ophthalmology, plastic surgery, orthopedics, and med spa practices. Nextech reports serving more than 16,000 medical practices with over 9,000 providers and 50,000+ office staff. The company was acquired by Francisco Partners, then by Thomas H. Lee Partners in 2019 (~$500M), and most recently by TPG Capital in 2023 for $1.4 billion, indicating significant scale in the specialty EHR market.

Nextech won the 2024 Best in KLAS: Ambulatory Specialty EHR award and the 2026 Best in KLAS for Ambulatory Ophthalmology Solutions. The company's ophthalmology capabilities were bolstered by its 2014 acquisition of MDIntelleSys, whose IntelleChartPRO cloud-based EHR was the leading cloud EHR for eye care at the time of acquisition. Nextech holds the American Academy of Dermatology's (AAD) DataDerm Gold Recognition for its dermatology EHR.

## Product: Nextech Select and NexCloud

CHPL IDs: 11722 (without NewCropRx), 11723 (with NewCropRx)

### What It Is

Nextech Select and NexCloud represent two deployment models of the same integrated EHR and practice management platform. **Nextech Select** is the on-premise/server-based deployment, while **NexCloud** (launched in 2016) is the cloud-based SaaS version offering browser-based access from any device. Both share the same core functionality — the certification covers them jointly. The "with NewCropRx" variant (CHPL 11723) adds electronic prescribing capabilities via the NewCrop integration (from DrFirst), which enables CPOE for medications (criterion a)(1)) and drug interaction checking (criterion (a)(4)) along with electronic prescribing for controlled substances (EPCS).

The certified module is **not just an EHR** — it encompasses an integrated EHR + practice management + patient engagement platform. The product is a comprehensive specialty practice platform covering clinical documentation, scheduling, billing, revenue cycle management, patient portal, analytics, inventory, CRM, and marketing tools.

### Users & Market

**Primary specialties**: Dermatology, ophthalmology, plastic surgery, orthopedics, and med spas — these are the five specialties Nextech markets to. The CHPL SED description specifically lists "Dermatology, Ophthalmology, and Plastic Surgery."

**End users**: Physicians/providers, clinical staff (nurses, technicians), front-office staff (scheduling, check-in), billing and coding staff, practice managers/administrators, and patients (via portal).

**Clinical settings**: Primarily ambulatory specialty practices, ranging from solo practitioners to multi-location groups. The product also supports ambulatory surgery center (ASC) workflows, with integrated ASC charting and billing mentioned specifically for ophthalmology and plastic surgery.

**Market position**: Nextech is a mid-to-large specialty EHR vendor. The 2023 TPG acquisition at $1.4B valuation and 16,000+ practice customer base places it as a significant player in the specialty ambulatory EHR space. Key competitors include Modernizing Medicine (ModMed), PatientNow, and NextGen.

### Modules & Functionality

Based on vendor website product pages, press releases, partner listings, and third-party reviews, the following modules and capabilities are described:

**Clinical Documentation / EHR**
- Specialty-specific customizable charting templates (dermatology, ophthalmology, plastic surgery, orthopedics)
- "Single-page EHR" design — ophthalmology version shows all encounter data on one page
- Smart stamping for rapid documentation
- Layered drawing functionality (ophthalmology: drawings auto-generated from findings, modifiable across visits)
- Subspecialty flowsheets (e.g., Glaucoma Flowsheet, Cataract Surgery Planner)
- Problem lists, medication lists, allergy lists, family history
- Clinical decision support with real-time quality measure checking during exams
- MIPS/quality reporting performance monitoring with documentation alerts
- Lab orders and results management
- Referral management with automatic letter generation
- CCDA support for care transitions with non-Nextech providers
- Shared care/referral network enabling data exchange between Nextech practices

**E-Prescribing (NewCropRx variant)**
- Electronic prescribing via NewCrop (DrFirst) integration
- Controlled substance prescribing (EPCS)
- Drug-allergy and drug-drug interaction checking
- Prescription history and pharmacy integration via Surescripts

**Photo/Image Management**
- Integrated clinical photography capture and storage
- Before-and-after image comparison (plastic surgery, dermatology)
- Image markup and drawing tools
- Diagnostic image integration (ophthalmology: Topcon Harmony integration)

**Practice Management**
- Advanced scheduling with drag-and-drop, multi-provider, multi-location support
- Patient intake and digital check-in (claimed 97% reduction in check-in time)
- Insurance eligibility verification (electronic)
- Patient demographics and registration
- Appointment reminders and confirmations

**Billing & Revenue Cycle Management**
- Insurance billing with integrated EHR coding
- Cosmetic/elective procedure billing (quotes, packages, gift cards, memberships)
- Claims submission, scrubbing, and tracking (via TriZetto integration)
- E-Billing, E-Eligibility, E-Remittance
- Payment posting automation
- Revenue cycle management (Assembly Health as exclusive RCM partner)
- Integrated payment processing (credit card, wireless/tap-to-pay, recurring payments)
- ASC billing integration
- Payment reconciliation

**Patient Portal & Engagement**
- Patient portal with online scheduling, bill pay, medical record access
- Mobile app for patients
- Two-way texting
- Telehealth/telemedicine via integrated video conferencing
- TouchMD patient engagement platform (Nextech product)

**CRM & Marketing**
- Nextech CRM for lead tracking and conversion
- Marketing campaign tracking and ROI analysis
- Automated email/call triggers based on patient data
- Lead source attribution

**Analytics & Reporting**
- Advanced Analytics platform connecting PM and EHR data
- Customizable dashboards with pre-built KPIs
- Revenue performance tracking (year-over-year, physician comparison)
- Operational analytics (appointment volume, patient trends, ICD-10 patterns)

**Inventory Management**
- Real-time product and supply tracking
- Cost and supplier pricing monitoring

**Compliance & Reporting**

- Public health reporting (certified for f)(5) — Electronic Case Reporting)

### Data & Content

Based on the features and modules described above, the product manages extensive data categories:

**Clinical data**: Patient demographics, encounter notes/documentation, problem lists, medication lists, allergy lists, family history, vital signs, lab orders and results, clinical decision support alerts, referral letters, care plan data, CCDA documents, quality measure data.

**Specialty clinical data**: Ophthalmic exam data (visual acuity, IOP, slit lamp findings, fundus findings), glaucoma tracking data, cataract surgery planning data, dermatological exam findings, clinical photographs/images with annotations, before-and-after comparisons, layered clinical drawings, diagnostic imaging data (via integrations).

**Prescription data** (NewCropRx variant): Prescription history, medication orders, drug interaction data, pharmacy information, controlled substance prescribing records.

**Administrative/scheduling data**: Appointment schedules, patient check-in/check-out records, wait times, appointment confirmations, provider schedules.

**Financial/billing data**: Insurance claims, billing codes (CPT, ICD-10), payment records, accounts receivable, eligibility verification results, cosmetic quotes and packages, gift card balances, membership/subscription records, payment plans, credit card transaction data, reconciliation records, RCM workflow data.

**Patient engagement data**: Patient portal messages, patient-completed forms, telehealth session records, two-way text messages, appointment self-scheduling records.

**CRM/marketing data**: Lead records, marketing campaign data, communication logs (emails, calls), lead source tracking, conversion metrics.

**Analytics data**: KPI dashboards, performance reports, revenue trends, operational metrics, clinical compliance tracking.

**Inventory data**: Product inventory levels, supplier pricing, cost tracking, POS transaction records.

**Audit/compliance data**: Audit logs, user access records, HIPAA compliance records, MIPS performance data, public health reports.

**Notable**: The vendor website prominently describes billing, scheduling, CRM, marketing, inventory, and analytics as integrated features — not separate products. The patient portal is also integrated. This means the EHI export for the certified product should encompass all of these data types, since they are all part of the same product of which the certified Health IT Module is a component.

**Gaps in research**: The exact boundary between what is "Nextech Select/NexCloud" (the certified product) vs. add-on products like "Nextech Advanced Analytics," "TouchMD," and "Nextech CRM" is not entirely clear from public materials. The vendor markets them as part of a unified platform, but they could conceivably be separately licensed modules. The EHI export obligation covers data stored by the product "of which the Health IT Module is a part," so if these are sold and deployed as part of the same product, their data should be included.
