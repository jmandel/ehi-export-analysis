## Overview

Netsmart Technologies is a major healthcare IT company headquartered in Overland Park, Kansas, that develops and sells health information technology for electronic health records (EHRs) and health information exchanges (HIEs) serving the **behavioral health, human services, and post-acute care** markets. The company is the largest HIT vendor specifically focused on these sectors, serving over 500,000 users in more than 24,000 organizations across all 50 states, and impacting more than 25 million individuals nationwide. Netsmart has approximately 2,500 employees and estimated annual revenue of approximately $500-550M (with ~$250M EBITDA reported in 2024).

**History:** The company's technology roots trace back to 1968, when Gerald O. Koop and John F. Phillips founded Creative Socio-Medics. Netsmart itself was created in September 1992 as a holding company originally known as Medical Services Corporation. It acquired Creative Socio-Medics in 1994, and went through several name changes before adopting "Netsmart Technologies" in February 1996. In August 1996, it held an IPO on NASDAQ under the symbol NTST. By 2005, Netsmart was the largest IT provider in the behavioral health sector, with revenues of approximately $50M. By 2011, the company had grown to 600 employees and $110M in revenue.

**Ownership:** Netsmart has passed through multiple private equity hands:
- **2006**: Bought out by Bessemer Venture Partners and Insight Venture Partners for $115M (taken private)
- **2010**: Acquired by Genstar Capital from Bessemer/Insight
- **2016**: Bought by GI Partners and Allscripts for $950M; Allscripts merged its homecare software business into Netsmart
- **2018**: GI Partners and TA Associates acquired Allscripts' stake; Netsmart has been owned by GI Partners and TA Associates since
- **2024**: Owners explored a sale potentially valuing Netsmart at $5 billion (including debt), working with Goldman Sachs and William Blair; as of early 2026, no completed sale has been publicly confirmed

**Key Acquisitions:**
- 1994: Creative Socio-Medics (founding technology)
- 2005: CMHC Systems of Ohio (community mental health centers, $18M) -- largest competitor at the time
- 2005: ContinuedLearning LLC and Addiction Management Systems
- 2011: Sequest Technologies (child/family services and substance use treatment)
- 2016: HealthMEDX (long-term care EHR)
- 2016: Allscripts homecare business (merged as part of GI Partners acquisition)
- 2017: DeVero (home health and hospice technology)
- 2019: McBee (250-employee healthcare consulting firm)
- 2020: Quality In Real Time (QIRT) (healthcare IT, New York)
- 2022: TheraOffice (physical therapy EMR/practice management, Chicago area)
- 2023: Netalytics (addiction treatment solutions, including Methasoft)

### What It Is

myEvolv is Netsmart's **behavioral health and human services EHR** -- a fully web-based, ONC-certified electronic health record with comprehensive clinical, financial, and operational features designed specifically for organizations serving specialized populations. Unlike myAvatar (Netsmart's other behavioral health EHR targeting larger psychiatric/addiction organizations), myEvolv focuses on the broader human services ecosystem:

- **Behavioral Health** (community mental health, outpatient behavioral health)
- **Child and Family Services** (foster care, child welfare, family preservation, early intervention)
- **Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities (IDD)**
- **Autism Services** (including Applied Behavior Analysis programs)
- **Addiction Treatment** (including Medication-Assisted Treatment/MAT)
- **Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinics (CCBHCs)**
- **Community-Based Outpatient Providers**
- **Foster Care**
- **Juvenile Justice Systems**
- **Social Services**

myEvolv is a cloud-based SaaS platform that is accessible from any browser and also has iOS and Android mobile apps. The certified edition (version 11.0) carries a broad ONC certification covering 38 criteria, including clinical data management (a)(1)-(a)(5), (a)(12), (a)(14); transitions of care (b)(1)-(b)(3); EHI export (b)(10)-(b)(11); clinical quality measures (c)(1)-(c)(3); privacy/security (d)(1)-(d)(9), (d)(12)-(d)(13); patient portal/VDT (e)(1), (e)(3); public health reporting (f)(1), (f)(2), (f)(5); FHIR API access (g)(2)-(g)(7), (g)(9)-(g)(10); and direct messaging (h)(1).

The system incorporates state-specific clinical records initiatives such as the New York State Clinical Records Initiative (NYSCRI) and the Massachusetts Standardized Documentation Project (MSDP), indicating significant deployments in these states.

### Users & Market

myEvolv targets **mid-market and larger behavioral health and human services organizations**. Day-to-day users include:

- **Behavioral health clinicians** (psychologists, therapists, addiction specialists, social workers, ARNPs, psychiatrists) performing clinical documentation and treatment planning
- **Community-based direct service providers** documenting services in the field via the mobile application
- **Case managers and care coordinators** tracking client services across programs and providers
- **Foster care specialists** managing foster parent applications, certifications, and child placements
- **ABA therapists and autism services staff** (via RethinkBH integration) performing behavioral data collection
- **Medication management staff** using eMAR and e-prescribing
- **Billing and administrative staff** managing complex Medicaid, Medicare, and commercial claims
- **Organizational administrators** configuring workflows and running reports

**Market Position:** Netsmart (across all its products) is recognized as the **#1 behavioral health EHR vendor** according to Black Book Research, which has given Netsmart top rankings for 9+ consecutive years. In 2025, Black Book surveyed 1,308 behavioral health professionals and 152 non-practitioner professionals and Netsmart maintained its top-rated position across key areas including revenue cycle management, clinical documentation, and patient management. Netsmart has received 26+ Black Book awards across behavioral health, geriatrics, long-term care/post-acute care, home health, and hospice.

However, KLAS Research presents a more nuanced picture -- about two-thirds of surveyed Netsmart customers have said they would not buy the same product again, citing development pace and support concerns (per the myUnity research). This suggests an industry-dominant vendor where customers sometimes feel locked in.

**Notable Deployments:**
- Arrow Child and Family Ministries -- uses myEvolv for comprehensive foster care and child services records
- Rainbows United -- uses myEvolv for developmental disability and children's services
- Missouri Behavioral Health Council -- implemented Netsmart CareManager for CCBHC population health management across the state
- Organizations incorporating NYSCRI (New York) and MSDP (Massachusetts) standardized documentation

**Competitors** in the behavioral health/human services EHR space include: Qualifacts (CareLogic), Core Solutions, Streamline Healthcare Solutions, Welligent, Valant, NextStep Solutions, TherapyNotes, ICANotes, and BestNotes. Larger general EHR vendors (Epic, Oracle/Cerner) also serve behavioral health but with less specialization.

### Modules & Functionality

myEvolv is organized around clinical, financial, and operational workflows. Key modules and capabilities found in vendor materials and review sites:

**Clinical Documentation:**
- Configurable clinical templates aligned to evidence-based practices
- Structured assessments and evaluation tools
- Progress notes with electronic signatures
- Real-time access to individual records from the office or the field
- Treatment planning tools for developing comprehensive, individualized treatment plans based on diagnosis, presenting issues, and goals
- Evidence-based treatment implementation support
- Clinical decision support
- Outcome tracking -- monitoring effectiveness of services by individual, staff, program, and agency
- Immunization tracking
- Allergy tracking
- Lab integration

**Medication Management:**
- Electronic Medication Administration Record (eMAR)
- E-prescribing (via OrderConnect platform)
- Medication history display
- Medication error reduction through centralized data

**Scheduling & Case Management:**
- Appointment scheduling and management
- Referral management
- Case coordination across programs, providers, and communities
- Milestone views for tracking required tasks (e.g., foster care applications, certifications)

**Billing & Revenue Cycle:**
- Automatic claim creation from clinical notes and placement/service data
- Payer rate determination based on coordination of benefits
- Medicare and state Medicaid billing support (complex multi-payer rules)
- Electronic claims submission with real-time monitoring
- Integrated accounts receivable/accounts pending (AR/AP)
- Financial data exportable to general ledger systems
- Integrated credit card payments via CardConnect

**Patient/Consumer Engagement:**
- Patient portal access (certified under (e)(1) and (e)(3) -- view, download, transmit)
- myHealthPointe consumer engagement software for provider-patient communication
- Appointment scheduling and reminders via the Netsmart app
- Lab results access for patients
- Electronic appointment scheduling and alerts

**Reporting & Analytics:**
- Standard reports covering client demographics, service utilization, and billing activity
- Clinical outcome and performance metric reporting
- Performance-based care model data collection
- Service effectiveness tracking by individual, staff, program, and agency
- CCBHC quality measure reporting
- Clinical quality measures (certified CQMs include CMS2v6, CMS68v6, CMS69v5, CMS117v5, CMS124v5, CMS125v5, CMS137v5, and others)

**Mobile & Offline:**
- **myEvolv Anywhere** (CarePOV) -- disconnected mobile application for community-based direct service providers
- Supports documentation in connected or disconnected environments
- Automatically re-syncs with the EHR once internet connection is restored
- Manages appointments, documents services, assessments, and tasks
- Captures session data, data collection, and electronic signatures on tablets
- Available on iOS and Android

**Specialized Capabilities:**
- **Foster Care Management**: Data collection for foster parents from application through recruitment, training, and re-certification; milestone tracking for CPR, first aid, foster care parenting courses
- **ABA/Autism**: Partnership with RethinkBH (RethinkFirst) for integrated Applied Behavior Analysis clinical documentation, scheduling, treatment planning, data collection, and assessment (mobile-enabled)
- **Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)**: Specialized tracking for MAT programs in addiction treatment
- **CCBHC Support**: Tools meeting key quality measures for Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinics, including a national CCBHC outcomes and reporting data repository developed with the National Council for Mental Wellbeing
- **Telehealth/Telemedicine**: Direct care delivery capabilities
- **Document Management**: Storage and management of clinical documents

**Interoperability:**
- **Netsmart CareConnect** integration engine for cross-provider data exchange
- HL7 FHIR R4 certified endpoints (Provider Patient Access API, Payer Patient Access API)
- SMART on FHIR support for patient-facing applications
- Carequality and HIE connectivity
- HL7 and ICD-10 compliance
- Direct messaging (certified (h)(1))
- Transitions of care document exchange (certified (b)(1)-(b)(3))
- Public health reporting: immunization registries (f)(1), syndromic surveillance (f)(2), electronic case reporting (f)(5)

### User Reviews & Feedback

**Overall Ratings:**
- Capterra: ~3.0 out of 5 stars
- EMRSystems: 4.0 out of 5 stars (4 reviews)
- Black Book Research: #1 ranked behavioral health EHR for 9+ consecutive years

**Commonly Cited Strengths:**
- Built specifically for behavioral health -- covers progress notes, treatment planning, and Medicaid billing well
- High configurability for varied organizational workflows and diverse funding requirements
- Custom templates and streamlined documentation
- Smooth navigation and easy client data input
- Clear medication history display supporting safer prescribing
- Integrated clinical-to-billing workflows reducing manual data entry
- Multi-platform accessibility (web, mobile)
- System flexibility for managing diverse programs and funding streams
- 100% usability testing success rate and 93% satisfaction rate cited by vendor
- Long-term users report the software evolves well to meet industry changes

**Commonly Cited Weaknesses:**
- **Implementation**: Described as "slow, cumbersome" with "no one owning the process" on Netsmart's end; poor project management; data conversion during implementation missed important information requiring manual re-entry
- **Reporting**: No "canned" reports for commonly needed tasks; each report must be individually crafted and cannot be shared between users; reports do not export to Excel well
- **Customer Support**: Described as "very poor" with staff "not trained properly"; slow response times; inadequate training and support materials post-go-live
- **Billing Compliance**: Difficulty implementing billing regulations; help for the financial/billing side described as inadequate
- **System Performance**: Some reports of slowness and crashes; UI described as "clunky" with navigation complaints
- **Training**: Insufficient training materials and guidance after implementation

### Netsmart Product Ecosystem

myEvolv fits within the broader **Netsmart CareFabric** platform, which is described as "a tightly woven framework of innovative clinical and business solutions and services that supports integrated, coordinated delivery of health services across the spectrum of care." Key products in the ecosystem:

| Product | Focus Area | Description |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| **myAvatar** | Behavioral Health (Enterprise) | Larger-scale behavioral health EHR for psychiatric hospitals, substance abuse providers, methadone clinics, CMHCs, managed care, and state mental health systems. 20+ modules. Offered on-premise or cloud. More enterprise-oriented than myEvolv. |
| **myEvolv** | Human Services / BH | Behavioral health and human services EHR for child/family, IDD, autism, addiction, CCBHC. Web-based SaaS. Mid-market focus. |
| **myUnity** | Post-Acute Care | Unified post-acute EHR spanning home health, hospice, palliative, SNF, senior living, personal care, adult day. |
| **TheraOffice** | Physical Therapy | EMR and practice management for physical, occupational, speech, and behavioral therapy practices. Acquired 2022. |
| **GEHRIMED** | Geriatric Medicine | Geriatric EHR and practice management for long-term post-acute providers. |
| **myInsight** | Public Health | Cloud-based public health EHR for immunizations, family planning, STI, TB, maternal/child health. |
| **myVRS** | Vital Records | Vital records management system for entry, validation, registration, and issuance of vital event records. |

**Cross-Platform Solutions:**
- **CareConnect**: Integration engine for interoperability, HIE connectivity, FHIR R4 APIs, Carequality
- **CareConnect Inbox**: Secure messaging
- **CareManager**: Population health management platform (EHR-agnostic)
- **OrderConnect**: E-prescribing platform
- **Bells AI**: AI-powered documentation assistant
- **AlphaCoding**: Automated coding
- **myHealthPointe**: Consumer engagement platform
- **Mobile Caregiver+**: Electronic visit verification (EVV)
- **CareRouter**: Mobile dispatch for workforce management
- **Plexus**: Professional services (RCM, IT managed services, cloud hosting, consulting)
- **RevConnect**: Clearinghouse and eligibility management
- **AlphaCollector**: Collections automation

**myAvatar vs. myEvolv Positioning:** Both are behavioral health EHRs, but they serve different market segments. myAvatar targets larger enterprise behavioral health organizations (psychiatric hospitals, state systems, large CMHCs) with 20+ modules and on-premise/cloud deployment options. myEvolv targets the broader human services market (child/family, IDD, autism, addiction) with a purely web-based SaaS model and more focus on community-based and outpatient settings. Both are certified under the 2015 Edition Cures Update.
