Overview
Cx360 V7 meets the certification criterion §170.315(b)(10) Electronic Health
Information export by implementing the CCD Export for Single Patient and Bulk
Export. This helps the clinical users to generate the Clinical Care Document for a
single patient or for a group of patients in accordance with the standards
specified in § 170.205(a)(4) HL7® Implementation Guide for CDA® Release 2:
Consolidated CDA Templates for Clinical Notes (US Realm), Draft Standard for
Trial Use Release 2.1, August 2015
• Single Patient Export and Bulk Patient Export in the CCD format which can be
printed in Adobe PDF format.
• Ability to download the Images / Clinical notes on demand and can be printed
in Adobe PDF format.

Single Patient Export

Users can select one or more sections to be printed in the CCD of the client / patient via
the Patient Summary Report Screen . Print button will print the Care document in Adobe
PDF Format which is human readable as well as in CCD format.

Bulk Patients Export
Users can select more than one client / patient based on the provider or appointments via
CCDA Management utility . Generate CCD button will print the Care document in Adobe PDF
Format which is human readable and CCD format for all the selected patients.
