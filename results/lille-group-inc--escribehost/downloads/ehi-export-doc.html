escribeHOST EHI Export Format Documentation

The escribeHOST EHI Table Exports comprise electronic health information (EHI) from a patient's Electronic Health Record (EHR) in a machine-readable, comma-separated value (CSV) file format specific to escribeHOST, reflecting the internal tables structure.

This single-page document encompasses all the EHI tables used to store information regarding a patient's EHI.

It is important to note that the escribeHOST EHI Table Exports only contain structural data. Additional files related to a patient's data, such as PDFs or images, are also included in the patient export files.

Exported Files

Documents or images cannot be presented in a CSV format. Although these files may be referenced within the Electronic Health Information (EHI) Tables, the actual files will be accessible as a part of the generated export package.

The generated export package may encompass a variety of binary files, such as PDFs or images, especially:

  • Encounter PDFs
  • Scanned Cards
  • PHR Sent Messages
  • PHR Received Messages
  • PHR Documents
  • CCDS Imported Documents
  • Patient Photos
