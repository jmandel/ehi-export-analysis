Cost Transparency:
Spark 7.1 requires software license fees, implementation fees for installation/training, and annual maintenance fees post go-live.

Additional Software Required: Meaningful Use users will require NewCrop Core ePrescribing. CorrecTek clients pay a one-time ePrescribing fee and a monthly service fee per prescriber. In addition, EMR Direct Interoperability Engine may also be required.
