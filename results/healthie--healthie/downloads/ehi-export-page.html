(b)(10) Electronic Health Information Export

This document describes Healthie’s support for ONC §170.315(b)(10) Electronic Health Information export.

Per (b)(10) ONC Certification, Healthie supports two types of electronic health information exports:

  1. Single patient
  2. System/patient population

IN THIS ARTICLE:

  • What is Electronic Health Information (EHI)?
  • Generating an Export
  • Export Format

What is Electronic Health Information (EHI)?

Electronic Health Information (EHI) refers to “electronic protected health information” (ePHI) to the extent that it would be included in a designated record set as defined:

  • Designated record set means:
    • A group of records maintained by or for a covered entity that is:
      • The medical records and billing records about individuals maintained by or for a covered health care provider
      • The enrollment, payment, claims adjudication, and case or medical management record systems maintained by or for a health plan; or
      • Records used, in whole or in part, by or for the covered entity to make decisions about individuals.

For purposes of this paragraph, the term record means any item, collection, or grouping of information that includes protected health. EHI does not include psychotherapy notes as defined in 45 CFR 164.501 or information compiled in reasonable anticipation of, or for use in, a civil, criminal, or administrative action or proceeding.

The EHI definition represents the same ePHI that an individual would have the right to access under the HIPAA Privacy Rule.

Generating an Export


A user can initiate a single-patient export by navigating to a patient’s profile and viewing the Charting section. The report can be generated by an organization member with the “Can generate organization report” setting enabled or by a non-organization user.


System exports can only be initiated by an administrator. The patient population data export is handled by the Healthie Support team as it depends on size of data, time and efforts required to manage the server resources. Please email hello@gethealthie.com with the subject line “Patient Population b10 export request” to initiate the process.

Export Format


Overview:

The EHI Export process generates CSV files, PDF files, and a folder per patient that are presented as a compressed zip file.

For the patient population export, the zip file contains one directory for each patient. The directories are named <FirstName><LastName><uniqueidentifier> . The contents of each directory are the same as the individual patient export. Further detail on each file is below.

Within the zip file the following csv files, PDFs, folder, and HTML page are generated per patient:
