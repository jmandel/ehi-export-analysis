# Adaptamed, LLC — Product Research

Researched: 2026-02-16
Developer website: https://ehryourway.com/

## Overview

Adaptamed, LLC is a privately held, family-owned company based in San Diego, California (6699 Alvarado Rd Suite 2301, San Diego, CA 92120). The company does business as "EHR Your Way" and was founded in 2008 by Dr. Kumara Prathipati and Dr. Joseph Reddy, who between them had approximately 70 years of clinical practice experience. The company reports having roughly 65 programmers and approximately 75 employees total, with estimated revenue in the $5–14 million range (sources vary). Adaptamed is a Delaware LLC.

EHR Your Way is a behavioral health-focused EHR platform serving small to mid-sized behavioral health practices, substance abuse treatment centers, and mental health organizations. The product is marketed as an integrated platform combining clinical documentation, billing/practice management, and patient engagement without requiring third-party integrations. The company offers a distinctive 180-day money-back guarantee. On Capterra, the product has 226 reviews with a 4.83/5 star rating, suggesting a loyal and satisfied user base. Notable customers mentioned include Gooden Center, All-Star Children's Foundation, University of Arizona, and Red River Behavioral.

## Product: EHR Your Way

CHPL IDs: 10757

### What It Is

EHR Your Way is a comprehensive, cloud-based electronic health record system designed specifically for behavioral health providers. It is ONC-certified (2015 Edition Cures Update) and SOC 2 certified. The certified module appears to be the entire product — there is no indication of separate modules sold independently. The product is described as covering both inpatient and outpatient behavioral health settings, and the company claims over 5,000 features.

The CHPL certification is broad: 40+ criteria spanning clinical data ((a)(1)–(a)(5), (a)(10), (a)(12)–(a)(14)), transitions of care ((b)(1)–(b)(3)), patient portal ((e)(1)–(e)(3)), clinical quality measures ((c)(1)–(c)(3)), public health reporting ((f)(1), (f)(5)), FHIR APIs ((g)(7), (g)(9), (g)(10)), and the EHI export criterion ((b)(10)). The SED intended user description specifies "Clinicians for inpatient or outpatient behavioral health facilities."

### Users & Market

**Target users**: Psychiatrists, therapists, counselors, substance abuse clinicians, social workers, case managers, billing staff, practice managers, and patients (via portal). The product serves both clinical and administrative staff.

**Clinical settings**:
- Outpatient mental health and substance abuse practices
- Inpatient behavioral health facilities
- Intensive outpatient programs (IOP)
- Community behavioral health centers (CCBHC)
- Autism spectrum disorder treatment
- Intellectual/developmental disability services
- Eating disorder treatment centers
- Homeless shelters (behavioral health)
- Correctional health
- Community health centers
- Speech therapy practices

**Practice size**: Ranges from small group practices (11–50 physicians) to larger organizations (50+ physicians). Primarily small to mid-sized organizations.

**Customer count**: Not explicitly stated on website. The 226 Capterra reviews and named customers suggest a moderate installed base.
