Data Export

Health IT developers that participate in the ONC Health IT Certification Program are subject to Conditions and Maintenance of Certification requirements introduced as part of the 2015 Edition Cures Update. The “Assurances” regulation requires Certified Health IT that electronically stores Electronic Health Information (EHI) to certify to the Cures Update § 170.315(b)(10) EHI Export criterion. Health IT developers certifying to this criterion must provide their customers with the capability to efficiently export single and patient population EHI in a secure and timely manner
