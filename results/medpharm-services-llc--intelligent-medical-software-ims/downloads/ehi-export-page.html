EHI Export

170.315(b) (10) Electronic Health Information Export

IMS Software enables the export of electronic health information (EHI) for an individual patient and the broader patient population in different export formats:

﻿

CCDA Format: 

IMS Software supports the efficient bulk export of HL7 CCDA XML files, ensuring compliance with the requirements of the United States Core Data for Interoperability (USCDI), Version 1.


Sections of EHI exported in CCDA:


  • Assessment
  • Clinical Notes
  • Durable Medical EquipMent
  • Encounters
  • Family History
  • Functional Status
  • Goals
  • Health Concerns
  • Immunization
  • Laboratory Values/Results
  • Medications
  • Medication Allergies
  • Payer
  • Plan of Treatment
  • Problems
  • Procedures 
  • Reason for Referral 
  • Smoking Status and Tobacco Use
  • Vital Signs


The specifications for the CCDA can be accessed from the HL7 website:

HL7 CCDA Specifications


FHIR Format: 

IMS Software also supports HL7® Version 4.0.1 FHIR® Release 4, October 30, 2019, FHIR® US Core Implementation Guide STU V3.1.1, HL7 FHIR Bulk Data export. 

For detailed specifications, please refer to 170.315(g)(10) for the Standardized API for patient and population services Specifications.
