Additional costs & limitations:
To achieve complete incentive program attestation using RevolutionEHR, ongoing monthly or annual costs are required to support RevDirect and RXNT. RevDirect provides Secure Electronic Messaging which supports the ability for users to securely send and receive direct messages containing clinical data with unlimited transactions. RXNT provides electronic prescribing, drug interaction & drug allergy checking solutions by subscribing to a yearly fee/per provider for unlimited transactions.
