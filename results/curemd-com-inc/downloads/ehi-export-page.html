170.315(b)(10) EHI Export

The §170.315(b)(10) regulation outlines the requirements for Electronic Health Information (EHI) export, promoting seamless access to and sharing of both individual and population health data. It mandates that health IT products enable users to export patients' electronic health data whenever needed, thereby fostering better data management and patient care.

CureMD’s EHI Export functionality will provide the EHI in an electronic and computable format, using CSV (Comma Separated Values) files. Additionally, patients’ documents and images will be exported in their native formats. For more detailed information on the data included in the export, please refer to the link below.
