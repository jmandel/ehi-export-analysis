eDerm is ONC Certified

On October 25, 2019 eDerm Systems completed the ONC Health IT Certification for the eDerm Systems Version 2.8.0 product.

The eDerm Systems certification number is 15.04.04.2592.eDer.28.00.1.191025

eDerm Systems supports the CQM CMS68v8.

Modules Tested: 170.315 (a)(2, 5, 12, 14); (b)(1-2, 10-11); (c)(1); (d)(1-9, 12-13); (e)(1); (g)(2-7, 9-10) 

Additional Software: NLM API, Updox

This Health IT Module is compliant with the ONC Certification Criteria for Health IT and has been certified by an ONC-ACB in accordance with the applicable certification criteria adopted by the Secretary of Health and Human Services. This certification does not represent an endorsement by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.
