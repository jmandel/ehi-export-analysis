Resource Profile: Observation

Official URL: https://ehi-export.pointclickcare.com//StructureDefinition/observation Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-02-04 Computable Name: EHIObservation

Measurements and simple assertions made about a patient, device or other subject. This includes vitals, occupation, smoking status, pregnancy status and pregnancy intent.
