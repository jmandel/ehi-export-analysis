Resource Profile: Medical Device

Official URL: https://ehi-export.pointclickcare.com//StructureDefinition/medicalDevice Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-02-04 Computable Name: EHIMedicalDevice

A type of a manufactured item that is used in the provision of healthcare without being substantially changed through that activity. The device may be a medical or non-medical device.

Usages:

  • This Resource is not used by any profiles in this Implementation Guide
