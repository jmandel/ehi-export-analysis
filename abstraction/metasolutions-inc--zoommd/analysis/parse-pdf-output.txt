General Export Information:
The ZoomMD application will support export of electronic health information (EHI) for an individual
patient as well as multiple patients. A ZIP file will be provided for each export which is having single
or multiple patient folders separately containing sub-folders of patient clinical documents and all
accessible EHI information.
