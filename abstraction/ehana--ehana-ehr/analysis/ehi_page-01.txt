Electronic Health Information (EHI) Export

Electronic Health Information

eHana supports the export of electronic health information (EHI) in accordance with §170.315(b)(10) -
Electronic Health Information export of the ONC 2015 Edition Cures Update Certification Criteria using
the CDA R2 format

Format

Electronic patient health information is available for export, in XML format, using the Consolidated-Clinical

Document Architecture (CCDA). eHana updates the CCDA in accordance with the HL7 Clinical Document

Architecture standard

Resource Types and Elements
