                 Patient EHI Data Export Overview and Instructions

     Your Patient Electronic Health Information (EHI) files are all sent via “Email/Print of your
     Chart” along with the option to Download the CCDA file through our Criterions Electronic
     Health Records System. Your emailed or printed chart will include your chart information
     that was stored by the practice.



     How to read your CCDA:



     A CCDA (Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture) file is an XML-based standard
     for the electronic exchange of structured clinical healthcare information. It's used to
     share patient summary information, including problems, medications, allergies, and
     procedures, among healthcare providers. CCDA files are often used for interoperability
     between different electronic health record (EHR) systems. You can use this file to send
     information to another doctor you'd like to see. If any of the fields are blank that is okay
     and not an error.

     Your CCDA file will include information on all of the following:


     •    ALLERGIES
     •    ASSESSMENT
     •    CONSULT NOTE
     •    DIAGNOSTIC IMAGING STUDY
     •    ENCOUNTERS
     •    FUNCTIONAL STATUS
     •    GOALS
     •    HEALTH CONCERN
     •    HISTORY AND PHYSICAL
     •    IMMUNIZATIONS
     •    LABORATORY REPORT NARRATIVE
     •    MEDICAL EQUIPMENT
     •    MEDICATIONS
     •    MENTAL STATUS
     •    PLAN OF CARE
     •    PROBLEM LIST
     •    PROCEDURES
     •    PROGRESS NOTE
     •    REASON FOR REFERRAL
     •    RESULTS
     •    SOCIAL HISTORY
     •    VITAL SIGNS
