Multi-Factor Authentication

Protecting Patient health information is of critical importance to practices. To ensure that each practice’s
information is as secure as possible the Criterions EHR 4 offers Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) through
SMS and/or email based on a practice’s configuration. When activated, MFA requires the entry of a unique
one-time password (OTP) sent via SMS/email to validate the unique user after entry of username and
password. Any user who cannot supply the MFA OTP code will be denied entry to the system in its
entirety. Upon login, the Criterions EHR requires additional MFA compliance for the transmission of
Electronically Prescribed Controlled Substances (EPCS). This additional layer of security ensures that
controlled medications are not transmitted without MFA of the submitting physician. Criterions Portal
applications support MFA through SMS and/or email OTP.


EHI

The Criterions EHR allows customers to export industry-standard patient information in XML format using
CCDA architecture and our Email/Print Chart functionality. Exports can be performed without intervention
from programming or Criterions Software, Inc and without charge for individual patients, groups of
patients, or automated scheduled export. Regular updates are performed in compliance with standards as
found at https://www.hl7.org/implement/standards/ .
