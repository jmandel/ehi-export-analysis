                              Novoclinical Data Export Format

C-CDA (Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture) – Each patient will have a C-CDA document in the
exported data.

CSV – Comma separated value, used for patient demography, Appointments.



Data export can be done by clinic administrators by navigating to Communication > Data Export

The below page opens and let you choose the specific patient export or group export of all or multiple
patients. Once exported administrator will be able to export the data in a ZIP file which will contact the
CCD-A, CSV file.
