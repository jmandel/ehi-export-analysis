Lunar EHI Export Overview
Lunar’s EHI Export functionality enables healthcare organizations to extract Electronic Health
Information (EHI) from the Lunar Cloud Platform in compliance with §170.315(b)(10) of the ONC
2015 Edition Cures Update. The platform supports single-patient and bulk-patient data extraction
in the form of an “export zip” file containing machine-readable files, enabling authorized users to
access and manage the designated record set as defined under 45 CFR 164.502.

The Lunar Cloud Platform leverages the C-CDA (Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture)
format for the majority of patient EHI, adhering to widely recognized industry standards for health
data interoperability. The C-CDA XML files provide structured and comprehensive representations
of clinical information, facilitating the smooth transfer and usability of EHI across systems.

In addition to the C-CDA format, certain EHI data - such as images, scanned documents, and
multimedia files - are included as comma-separated values (csv) files. These supplemental files
ensure a complete and cohesive representation of the patient’s health information.

The content and format of the exported EHI may vary based on:

   ●​ Lunar Cloud Platform Configuration: Organization-specific settings and workflows.
   ●​ Implementation Details: Standard implementations include the Lunar Clinical Suite, Lunar
       Revenue Suite, Lunar Lab Suite, Lunar IT Suite, Lunar Operations Suite, Lunar Analytics
       Suite, and Lunar Patient Suite.
   ●​ Software Version: The current version of the Lunar Cloud Platform in use.
   ●​ Organizational Practices: Data documentation, input workflows, and system usage.

C-CDA Format Guide
The Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture (C-CDA) is a standardized format developed by
HL7 (Health Level Seven International) to enable the exchange of structured clinical documents
across different health systems. It serves as the foundation for the majority of patient data
exported from the Lunar Cloud Platform.

C-CDA is an XML-based format that organizes and represents patient health information in a
machine-readable, interoperable structure. It provides a standardized way to store and share
critical health data, ensuring consistency and compatibility between EHR systems.

The Lunar Cloud Platform uses CDA Release 4.1. The official HL7 documentation for C-CDA can be
found at the following URL:

https://www.hl7.org/implement/standards/product_brief.cfm?product_id=447

C-CDA Document Sections
The following sections outline the core components of a C-CDA document from the Lunar Cloud
Platform, each representing a specific aspect of a patient’s health record. These sections are
designed to provide a clear and organized summary of clinical information, including encounters,
medications, procedures, and more.

Additionally, the last section, Supplemental Data, is a proprietary Lunar data format that
supplements a patient’s C-CDA with any additional electronic health information in a series of
comma-separated values (csv) files that’s not captured by the standard C-CDA format.
