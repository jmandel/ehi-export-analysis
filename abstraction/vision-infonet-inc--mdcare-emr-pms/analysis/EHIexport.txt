Overview

MDCareEMRV5.1 meets the certification criterion §170.315(b)(10) Electronic Health Information - Export by implementing the
CCD Export for Single Patient and Bulk Patients. Based on specific user roles, this helps the clinical users to generate the Clinical
Care Document in accordance with the standards specified in § 170.205(a)(4) HL7® Implementation Guide for CDA® Release 2:
Consolidated CDA Templates for Clinical Notes (US Realm), Draft Standard for Trial Use Release 2.1, August 2015


MDCareEMRV5.1 will generate complete electronic health information in C-CDA,XML and PDF formats.


To achieve this, we have implemented,
      Single Patient Data Export and Bulk Patients Data Export that can be in the CCD format and also can be printed in PDF
       format.
      Ability to download the Images / Clinical notes on demand and can be printed in PDF format.
